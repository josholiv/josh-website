Today, for me, the [Functional Neuroimaging of Memory lab](https://fnim.utdallas.edu) became the [Functional Neuroimaging of Memories lab](https://fnim.utdallas.edu/2026/06/25/farewell-to-our-dear-colleagues/). I've spent the last few weeks wrapping up my role as lab manager. Today was my last day, and I'll be transitioning over to starting my PhD in psychology later this summer at the University of California, Riverside. It's hard to put into words how profoundly grateful I am to have been part of the fNIM lab over the past few years. It's been a great time, and I could not have asked to spend it here alongside a better group of people. It's bittersweet to move on to a new chapter. But luckily, I have plenty of great memories to bring with me. I'm excited to see what the future brings!
